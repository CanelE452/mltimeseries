# Prospective decisions about further LoRA adaptation

User explicitly approved executing the three publication-path checks on 2026-09-11: predictive conditional differences, signal ablations/alternative explanations, and actual quality/cost against strong baselines. This is an authorized bounded research screen, not a promise of positive results or publication acceptance. Previous Study32 artifacts remain immutable.

## Claim and design rationale

Question: can a validation-only short paired continuation choose between keeping learned LoRA fixed, updating it further, and stopping, with better selected-checkpoint generalization and adaptation cost than tuned simple policies?

Keep Chronos-2, native pinball loss, rank8 alpha16 attention LoRA, the zero-output residual MLP533, context336/horizon48, batch8/micro4, clipping1, AdamW weight decay0, and resource guards from the verified Study32. Keep FULL90 and SPREAD30 to contrast contiguous full exposure with equal-range sparse exposure. RECENT30 is omitted from this bounded screen before seeing new outcomes, not selected out for poor performance. Use maximum180/60 updates respectively. Recipes0/1/2 are head/LoRA LR (1e-4,1e-4), (1e-4,3e-5), (3e-5,3e-5): recipe1 isolates LoRA LR with head LR fixed; recipe2 tests global LR sensitivity. The exact-count WIDE head from Study30 is a further strong control, not a claim of equal function class.

Alternatives: on/off contribution (cheap, but measures already learned dependence), a one-gradient Taylor proxy (cheaper but unverified for the large nonlinear/Adam continuation), and paired short rollouts (chosen because they measure actual adaptation). A low-probability analogy is a controller's safe trial pulse: act briefly and roll back before committing. It is not claimed as a new idea or as a proven mechanism. If the cost of probing erases its benefit, discard this candidate rather than making increasingly complex variants on the same test.

Prior foundations: [LoRA, ICLR2022](https://openreview.net/forum?id=nZeVKeeFYf9) provides low-rank updates; [Hyperband, JMLR2018](https://jmlr.org/papers/v18/16-558.html) already uses short training budgets for allocation; [AFLoRA, ACL2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-short.16/) already adaptively freezes projections while learning transformation vectors. Thus neither probing nor freezing alone is novelty. AFLoRA's [official implementation](https://github.com/zeyuliu1037/AFLoRA) changes the adapter parameterization as well as the freezing score: a score-only LoRA variant must not be called a faithful AFLoRA reproduction. [In-Context Fine-Tuning, ICML2025](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/faw25b.html) is another adaptation direction for time-series FMs; this controlled screen keeps the backbone/task representation fixed.

## Fixed pilot criteria and stop conditions

Differences are normalized by initial E loss;0.25%F0 is a prespecified practical band, not a significance/equivalence test or publication threshold.

1. Predictive conditional screen: candidate uses at least2 different actions and improves mean selected E by more than0.25%F0 against each of global constant, source constant and random. Report all12 action regrets against the retrospective three-action oracle; the oracle is not deployable. Report trial-V versus final-E utility, not only classification accuracy on ties.
2. Signal ablation screen: mean selected E improvement more than0.25%F0 over CURRENT_C and HEAD_PROBE. Report each source/seed, clipping and same-head-LR contrasts. A pooled win alone does not establish mechanism; report disagreement or LR dependence as a limitation.
3. Practical quality/cost screen: compare to tuned FIXED, tuned ES and the strongest development-selected baseline among FULL/HEAD/WIDE. Thus these are three required comparisons even if FIXED or ES has the best development score overall. For every test seed, either candidate regret<=0.25%F0 and adaptation time saving>5%, or candidate quality gain>0.25%F0 at no greater adaptation time. Also require no source-average regret>0.25%F0. Report all5 baseline comparisons and all failures. These are continuation rules only, not acceptance standards.

Report conditional paired moving-block bootstrap90% intervals for quality differences if computed: sample blocks of2 consecutive evaluation origins (eight-day spacing span),2000 draws, same draw across methods/seeds/conditions within each source. Keep sources fixed and label the interval as within-window uncertainty, not population-of-datasets inference. Seed results share targets and cannot be counted as independent time windows.

Run these three tests once. Do not retune on final E or create an inverse-C policy after viewing results. If any basic criterion fails, finish all three diagnostics/cost checks and close this candidate. A faithful closest-prior comparison (especially AFLoRA), a second FM and broader source/period replication become necessary if it survives; without those, do not claim a paper-ready method. Do not spend compute on porting a new adapter solely to rescue a candidate that already loses to a simple baseline.
